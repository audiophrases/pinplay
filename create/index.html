🎯 Levels

Tag questions with difficulty steps 1–12 for Adaptive and Climb, or keep CEFR levels on an existing quiz. Polls are skipped. Teacher-graded questions move the level once you grade them (Adaptive assignments only; PinPlay Cup and Climb skip them).

Difficulty steps (1 to 12) work in Adaptive and Climb. When a quiz has steps, both modes use them; questions without a step stay out of individual question selection. Without steps, existing CEFR levels still work. Students never see steps or levels.
